What this app does

This tracker helps drivers keep an eye on their on-duty hours against DOT Hours-of-Service limits. You log a Start and End time for each work session, subtract any Break minutes, and the app totals it up for the day and the week.

How to use it

  • Tap a day (Monday–Sunday) to expand it and enter your session times.
  • Add more than one session per day with "+ Add Another Session" if you clocked in/out more than once.
  • Enter Break minutes for that session — it's automatically subtracted from your on-duty total.
  • Tap "I'm Done for Today" to lock the day and see when you're legally allowed to clock in next.
  • Use Export at the bottom to save a CSV, a text report, or share/copy a quick summary.

What the limits mean

  • 14 hrs — max on-duty window in a day.
  • 11 hrs — max on-duty hours in a day (driving-focused, but this app tracks combined on-duty time since many drivers do both driving and other duties in the same shift).
  • 70 hrs — weekly on-duty cap.
  • 10 hrs off / 34 hrs reset — minimum rest before you can clock back in.
